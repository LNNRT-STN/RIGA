# KBC Ecosystem Challenge — Participant Instructions

## The challenge

**Design a scalable, customer-centered ecosystem that helps people move from intent to successful action.**

Your solution should help KBC understand what customers are trying to achieve, identify what prevents them from achieving it, and coordinate the services, partners, and actions needed to make progress.

The goal is **not** to build another isolated AI tool or simply improve a prediction model. The goal is to help KBC make the next important thing happen in a customer's life.

## Why this matters

Customers do not wake up wanting to do banking. They want to start a business, live more sustainably, build something, or achieve another meaningful goal. Banking is only one part of the journey.

Consider buying a home: the customer's intent is straightforward — *“I want a place of my own.”* But the journey involves loans, insurance, administration, notaries, public authorities, moving companies, and property websites. Customers are often left to coordinate these activities themselves.

AI can help, but a growing collection of disconnected AI tools can create even more complexity. Customers also need confidence about how their data is used and control over what services can do on their behalf.

KBC has an opportunity to bring these pieces together: trusted financial services, digital platforms, ecosystem partners, and customer-controlled data sharing.

## Your mission

Create a concept for an ecosystem that:

1. **Starts with a customer goal.** Identify a meaningful intent or life ambition, not just a banking transaction.
2. **Maps the full journey.** Show the steps customers must take, the friction they encounter, and the people or organizations involved.
3. **Removes obstacles.** Explain how KBC could connect services and partners to simplify coordination and help the customer act.
4. **Uses AI purposefully.** Describe where intelligence, personalization, or proactive assistance adds value, without adding unnecessary tools or complexity.
5. **Respects customer control.** Let customers choose how proactive KBC can be and what data is shared with KBC and ecosystem partners.
6. **Creates a triple win.** Demonstrate clear value for customers, KBC, and participating partners.
7. **Can scale.** Show how the ecosystem could grow beyond one narrow use case or a manual, one-off solution.

## Scope and constraints

- **Do not choose housing or mobility.** KBC already works in these ecosystems, so they are out of scope for this challenge.
- **Explore new domains.** Sustainability and entrepreneurship are suggested starting points, not mandatory choices.
- **Think beyond a standalone app, chatbot, or AI model.** Focus on an end-to-end ecosystem that turns an intention into an outcome.
- **Keep trust and consent central.** Customers should understand and control how their data is used and shared.
- **Make partner participation worthwhile.** Partners need a reason to contribute and a fair share of the value created.

## How to approach the challenge

### Step 1 — Choose an intent

Identify a specific customer ambition. Write it in the customer's voice:

> “I want to ______.”

Choose an ambition important enough to involve multiple steps, decisions, or service providers.

### Step 2 — Identify the barriers

Map the customer's journey from that intent to a successful outcome. Ask:

- What must the customer do?
- Where do delays, uncertainty, paperwork, or fragmented experiences occur?
- Which organizations are involved?
- What does the customer currently have to coordinate alone?

### Step 3 — Design the ecosystem

Show how KBC could bring together its capabilities, digital experiences, and external partners to remove those barriers. Describe the roles of each participant and how the experience feels coherent to the customer.

### Step 4 — Define proactive support and data controls

Explain where AI or automation could anticipate needs, propose next steps, or take action with permission. Specify what the customer can opt into, review, approve, or switch off, including data sharing with partners.

### Step 5 — Prove the triple win

Describe the benefit for each participant:

| Stakeholder | Value to demonstrate |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Less friction, greater control, and faster progress toward a meaningful goal |
| KBC | A stronger customer relationship and a useful role beyond individual financial transactions |
| Ecosystem partners | A clear incentive to participate and share in the value created |

### Step 6 — Show how it scales

Explain how the ecosystem could support more customers, scenarios, and partners without becoming another disconnected set of tools.

## What to present

Use the following as a suggested structure for your final concept:

1. **Customer ambition:** Who is the customer, and what do they want to achieve?
2. **Current journey:** What stands in their way today?
3. **Ecosystem vision:** How does the proposed experience work from intent to outcome?
4. **KBC's role:** Why is KBC well placed to orchestrate or enable it?
5. **Partners:** Who participates, what do they contribute, and why would they join?
6. **AI and data control:** What is proactive or automated, and what remains under customer control?
7. **Triple-win value:** What improves for the customer, KBC, and partners?
8. **Scalability:** How could the concept expand over time?

*This presentation outline is a practical interpretation of the speech, not an explicitly stated mandatory submission format.*

## Guiding question

**What progress is the customer trying to make, what stands in their way, and how could KBC remove those barriers?**

## What success looks like

The strongest concept is not necessarily the one with the most sophisticated prediction model. It is the one that convincingly helps KBC enable an important real-world outcome for its customers.
