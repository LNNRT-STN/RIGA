# Pitch script, Team Riga

Generated by the Claude app from `pitch_story.md`, `pitch_facts.md` and the live deck. Regenerated on every "update our presentation". Edits you make by hand are kept unless the story or facts change underneath them.

**Version:** 3 minutes (default until the pitch length is in `pitch_story.md`)
**Word budget:** about 390 words, spoken at about 130 words per minute. The demo uses fewer words, because clicks and loading take time.
**Status:** first draft. Every `[bracket]` comes from `pitch_story.md` or `pitch_facts.md` once they are filled in.

## Who does what

| Role | Person | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Speaker 1 | [name] | Opening, problem, solution, close |
| Speaker 2 | [name] | Demo story, how it works, impact |
| Demo driver | [name] | Drives the laptop, never speaks during the pitch |
| Questions lead | [name] | Picks who answers each question from the jury |

Two voices keep it clear. Two handovers is the maximum in three minutes.

## How to rehearse

Learn the first line, the last line and the two handovers by heart. Know the rest well enough to say it in your own words; reading a memorised script word for word sounds stiff.

Rehearse standing up, with a timer and the real laptop, at least five times. Record one run on a phone and watch it back. Speak slower than feels natural. A pause feels long to you, but it sounds confident to the jury.

Say "everyone" or "members of the jury", not "ladies and gentlemen" or "guys". Spell out every abbreviation the first time.
