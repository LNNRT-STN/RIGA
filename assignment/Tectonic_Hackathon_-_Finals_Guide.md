## Submission & judging

Submissions are done through the **Builderbase** platform. One of your team members logs in, collects the data and fills in the fields under *Overview*. You will need to:

- write a short description,
- make a pitch presentation (< 3 minutes),
- give a GitHub repo link, and
- give screenshots of the Aikido platform.

Our judges will score you on:

1. **Creativity:** how original is your idea? (25%)
2. **Technical ability:** does it work? (20%)
3. **Fit:** did you solve the challenge? (20%)
4. **Pitch quality:** how well did you present? (25%)
5. **Security:** how secure is it? (10%)

## KBC Challenge

### The opportunity

Customers often have to recognise a need, compare options and coordinate multiple providers themselves. KBC wants to become a trusted partner that understands intent, anticipates needs and connects the right services and partners to help customers achieve meaningful life or business outcomes.

### The challenge

Design a scalable ecosystem that helps customers move from intent to successful action.

**Your solution should:**

- Recognise signals of an emerging need, ambition, opportunity or challenge.
- Understand the customer context behind that intent.
- Activate relevant KBC capabilities, channels, expertise and ecosystem partners.
- Guide decisions and actions while keeping the customer in control.
- Deliver a measurable outcome and a sustainable triple win for customers, KBC and partners.

**Design principles:**

- Bold and differentiated, not a minor product improvement.
- Personalised and adaptive, yet scalable to millions of customers.
- Trustworthy, transparent and privacy-conscious.
- Commercially viable for KBC and attractive for participating partners.
- Grounded in a believable customer journey and demonstrated through a proof of concept.

### Building blocks and scope

Use KBC's banking, insurance, platforms, data, AI, advisory capabilities and ecosystem partnerships as building blocks. You may combine existing capabilities, connect multiple domains, introduce new partners or imagine new capabilities.

Possible domains (be bold): energy & sustainability, entrepreneurship & business growth, and travel.

> **Guiding question:** How can KBC help customers achieve important life and business goals before they realise they need help?

## SD Worx Challenge

### One more challenge

**Can your solution stay trusted without becoming another task?**

You started with one challenge:
**How might we turn fragmented organisational knowledge into a trusted shared resource?**
Now take your solution one step further.

Knowledge changes. People change roles. Expertise evolves. Context gets lost.

How can your solution stay **useful, reliable and up to date** without asking people to constantly document, update or maintain it?

### Your extra challenge

**Keep it trusted. Make it transparent.**
Think invisible UI, calm technology and frictionless experiences:

- Automate what can reasonably happen in the background
- Verify when human judgement adds real value
- Minimise unnecessary effort and attention
- Integrate naturally into existing ways of working
- Make it understandable when people need to know, decide or act

The technology should be almost invisible when things work and clear when people need to understand or act.

**Think beyond documents**
Sometimes the most useful answer is not in a document. It is knowing:

- Who has dealt with this before?
- Who really knows this customer or topic?
- Who knows how this actually works here?
- Who knows why we do things this way?

**For example**

**You are a new developer at SD Worx.**
You can look up how a build pipeline works. The harder questions are:
**"How do we do this here?", "Who should I talk to?"**

**You need to launch a marketing campaign.**
The harder questions might be:
**"Who has done this before?", "Who understands this audience?", "Who knows what actually worked?"**

Could your solution learn from signals already created through everyday work, instead of requiring employees to maintain another knowledge system?
**These examples are inspiration, not requirements. Solve the underlying problem.**

### Show us how

Ask yourselves:

1. What happens automatically?
2. When is human verification genuinely valuable?
3. How do you prevent maintenance from becoming a burden?
4. How does your solution fit naturally into the user's workflow?
5. How does your solution remain understandable without demanding constant attention?

The goal is not zero human involvement. The goal is zero unnecessary effort.

**Find it. Understand it. Trust it. Keep it trusted. Make it transparent. Make it effortless.**

## Technical partners

### Aikido

We are proud to partner with Aikido, the leading software security company. As part of the finals, your submission will need to have a security audit by Aikido. It will again weigh 10% of your submission assessment. Need help? Aikido is onsite to support you!

**1. Prepare your project repository**
Put your hackathon project in a GitHub repository. Make sure your latest project code is committed and pushed.
You must use the same project you are building for the hackathon. Unrelated projects, existing projects submitted as your own, or deliberately trivial projects created solely for this challenge are not eligible.

**2. Create a free Aikido account**
Go to [app.aikido.dev](https://app.aikido.dev) and create a free account using GitHub. Follow the onboarding steps to set up your Aikido workspace.

**3. Connect your repository**
Authorize Aikido to access your hackathon repository and select it for scanning. Once the initial scan is complete, open the findings in your dashboard.

**4. Review and fix vulnerabilities**
Review the vulnerabilities, their severity, and the remediation guidance in Aikido. Implement fixes to improve your score, then commit and push your updated code.
Start early to leave time for fixes and a completed scan of your latest code before the submission deadline.

**5. Submit your results**
By 16h, submit the following via Builderbase:

- Link to your GitHub repository.
- Screenshots of your final Aikido scan results, showing the repository name and remaining vulnerabilities.

## Rules & Fair Play

- **Build within the time limit.** Your project must be built during the official hackathon time slot.
- **Submit on time.** Submit one project per team through the official platform, with all required links.
- **Final means final.** No code changes or submission edits after your final submission. Judges assess that version.
- **Keep your GitHub repository public** and accessible until judging is complete.
- **Include a short README.** Explain your project, how to run it and anything unfinished.
- **Check your links.** Judges must be able to access your repository and other submitted materials.
- **Pitch presentations are PPT or PDF!**
- **Work as a team.** Collaborate with your registered teammates and take shared responsibility for your project.
- **Protect private information.** Never upload passwords, API keys or confidential data.
- **Respect everyone.** Harassment, discrimination, plagiarism and cheating are not acceptable.
- **Follow the organisers' instructions.** Judges' decisions are final. Rule violations may lead to disqualification.
