# From Intent to Impact

> **Design the next customer ecosystem.**

**Design a scalable ecosystem that helps customers move from intent to successful action.**

---

## 1. Build the Journey

Show how KBC can:

- **Recognise** an emerging need, ambition, opportunity or challenge
- **Understand** the context and intent behind it
- **Activate** the right services, expertise and partners
- **Guide** the customer through decisions and actions
- **Help deliver** a measurable outcome

---

## 2. Think Ecosystem

Start from the **customer's goal**, not from a banking product. Use KBC's existing capabilities as **building blocks, not boundaries**.

You can:

- Combine existing capabilities in new ways
- Connect different life and business domains
- Introduce new ecosystem partners
- Invent capabilities KBC does not have today

> Sustainability and entrepreneurship are **inspiration areas, not limitations**. Choose the life or business challenge where you believe KBC can create meaningful impact.

---

## 3. Make It a Triple Win

| Stakeholder | Win |
|---|---|
| **Customer** | The goal becomes easier, faster or better to achieve. |
| **KBC** | The solution strengthens the customer relationship and creates sustainable business value. |
| **Ecosystem** | Partners have a clear reason to participate and share in the value created. |
