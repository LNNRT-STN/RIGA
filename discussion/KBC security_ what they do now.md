# **KBC security: what they do now**

**Login & app protection**

* Device registration (one-time) so only your device can bank; a personal PIN that creates a unique digital signature; lockout after 3 wrong PINs; auto-logout after inactivity; encryption; built-in antivirus scanning for banking malware  
* Screenshots blocked on Android 13 and lower  
* PhoneCheck app built on Kaspersky's fraud-prevention SDK (Android & iOS)  
* You choose your own max daily transfer amount when registering KBC Mobile

**Scam-specific tools**

* **"Check je gesprek"**: a button in KBC Mobile/Touch to verify whether the person claiming to be KBC is really KBC, also usable when you call KBC yourself; works for phone, video calls and home visits  
* **Guardian Angel (Engelbewaarder)**: launched Feb 2026 as a Belgian first; a trusted person co-monitors suspicious transfers, aimed at "human takeover" fraud  
* **Verification of Payee**: IBAN–name check on all SEPA transfers within the EEA since 10 October 2025

**Monitoring & response**

* Calls you immediately if a transaction looks suspicious  
* Uses AI for fraud detection  
* Secure4u fraud department  
* Fraudstop (sector-wide, 078 170 170), 24/7, connects you to your bank's fraud team or helps block apps

**Insurance & education**

* KBC CyberSecure insurance: covers fraud/scam/identity theft losses from €100 to €25,000 per claim on one current account (around €7/month)  
* In-app scam news in KBC Mobile/Touch, plus kbc.be/secure4u  
* Advice to lower card and transfer limits; pointers to Safeonweb training and app

# **Market research: the threat today**

* Phishing losses in Belgium hit €93M in 2025, up from €49M in 2024, so roughly doubled  
* The CCB received more than 40,000 phishing reports per day in Q1 2026, a 50% increase year on year  
* Banks still catch, block or recover about 75% of phishing transfers, so about a quarter gets through  
* Top scams: safe-account fraud (phishing mail, then a call to move money to a "safe" account), spoofed KBC phone numbers, fake bank staff collecting cards and PINs at the door, screen-sharing requests, and WhatsApp "hi mum" fraud  
* Deepfakes: a convincing voice clone now needs only 3–5 seconds of audio
