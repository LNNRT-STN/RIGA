**How does the Trust System work**

1. Integrated in KATE  
2. (zero trust architecture)  
3. Human in the loop for automated actions  
4. Trust works both passively in kate you upload inoices, mails and check wether they are trustworthy. But also proactively: KBC trust within kate notify’s users of dangerous transactions behaviour before the user can execute them.  
5. Customer control: which data shared, ability to change it  
6. Triple win\! Customer: no scams, instant “is this real?” answers ;KBC: fewer PSR spoofing refunds, partner revenue, more trust; Partners: less brand abuse, fewer support calls, correct payments  
7. Scalability: Open protocol and API for any partner; new threats become new modules.  
8. Measurable outcome: € fraud prevented, % of payments to verified senders, time to answer “is this real?” (aim for under 5 seconds).   
9. Read in KBC security: what they do now (to know the current capabilities)  
10. Verified by kbc partner (how does it work and why will partners join, explain the model)
